I've been an avid fan of Dungeons and Dragons since i was in highschool. So much so that I've adopted a hobby of diorama building.
This piece in particular was constructed from cardboard, XPS foam, linnen, flour, glue, paint, clay, etc... and took upwards of 2 months to complete. I used this diorama for the final battle of a campaign I ran my sophomore year of college.
