The Arena of Valor is a tournament style event where teams fight eachother until only one is left standing! In my 5th Dungeons and Dragons campaign that I ran in college, my players concluded their journey on the Arena of Valor final stage. And to spice things up, I added some moving parts.
This project taught me the basics of the Arduino IDE as well as many lessons in combining aesthetics with functionality. This project hit a lot of roadblocks, but I'm ultimately really happy with the result.
