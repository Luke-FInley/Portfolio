In 2025 I took a class that honed my skillis in Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects, and Blender. This project was one of my absolute favorites of the class, inspired by one of my favorite movies of all time: Real Steel.
This project is based on the loosly mentioned backstory for one of the characters in the movie and is treated like my own version of a 'prequel'. This project predominantly improved my knowledge on Blender and After Effects.
